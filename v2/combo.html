The 6x engine

45¢ buffer.
6x exposure.
Zero margin calls.

You put in $1. The vault keeps ~45¢ as a USDC buffer and posts the rest as margin on Hyperliquid.
It opens ~$6 of BTC exposure. That's your 6x — long for iVOL, short for VOL.
Price moves, it re-sizes. Winning? Exposure goes up to stay 6x, so trends compound. Losing? Exposure shrinks, so you're never over-levered. The buffer funds margin so you never get a call.

What the buffer is not: it doesn't lock in profits or protect you. As the vault re-sizes, gains get re-levered at 6x — in the 2020 COVID sim, VOL peaked at +2,346% and was then wiped out on the bounce.*

Deep liquidity behind every token

Each vault holds its position as a perp on Hyperliquid's order book. Get in and out through a USDC pool on HyperEVM, or mint/redeem straight from the vault at NAV, up to the vault limit ($500k deposit cap per vault; sells above instant-sell capacity are refused, not queued).

Fordefi

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I get liquidated?

You have no liquidation price of your own. The vault manages margin, but the vault itself can be liquidated: a fast move against it can erase most or all of its value. Rough wipe-out move at 6x: ~17% against you.

Are those returns real?

No. Every backtest figure is simulated, not real performance: our model of the vault's documented rules on hourly BTC data, with flat funding and no slippage. Real vaults launched Sep 2026.

Does the 45¢ buffer lock in my gains?

No. It's what lets the vault hold ~6x without margin calls. Gains are re-levered as the vault re-sizes, and can be lost.

How do I get in and out?

Pay with USDC from HyperEVM, Arbitrum, Base and more. Trades go through a USDC pool on HyperEVM; if it's thin you mint (0.1%) or redeem (0.3%) at NAV from the vault, up to its limits.

Who controls the vault?

A Fordefi multi-sig (MPC). The owner can pause trading and upgrade code. Audited by Pashov.
