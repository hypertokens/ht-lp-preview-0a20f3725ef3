FAQ

Straight answers.

How do I buy?

Pay with USDC from HyperEVM, Arbitrum, Base and more. Trade the USDC pool on HyperEVM, or mint at NAV straight from the vault. One transaction; iVOL or VOL lands in your wallet.

How do I sell?

Sell into the pool any time, or redeem at NAV from the vault.

What does it cost?

Pool fee 0.3–1%, or 0.1% to mint and 0.3% to redeem at NAV, plus 2%/yr management inside NAV. Funding and trading costs sit inside NAV too; there's no margin for you to fund.

How often does it re-size?

Eligible about every 4 hours, or whenever leverage drifts 10% from target. On-chain and automatic.

Who runs the vault?

Vault admin sits behind a Fordefi MPC multi-sig. The position lives on Hyperliquid; the token is an ERC-4626 share on HyperEVM.

How big can I go?

Each vault takes deposits up to $500k.

Liquidation

You have no liquidation price of your own; the vault manages the margin. The vault's perp position can itself be liquidated by a fast enough move against it (roughly 17% at 6x), which would erase most or all of the token's value.

Sideways markets

Re-sizing has a cost when price chops, and NAV drifts down. Live on-chain NAV Oct 2–21 2026: iVOL −22.3%, VOL −26.4%, BTC −1.9%.

Margin reserve

The ~45¢ USDC margin reserve is working margin and is re-levered as the vault re-sizes. It does not protect value or lock in gains. Rebalancing is eligible, not guaranteed. Hedge figures are exposure at entry and drift as BTC moves.

Vault, access and fees

The vault owner (a Fordefi MPC multi-sig) can pause trading and upgrade code. $500k deposit cap per vault; trading and redemptions are subject to liquidity, vault limits and pauses, and sells above instant-sell capacity are refused. Fees: pool fee 0.3–1%, or 0.1% mint / 0.3% redeem at NAV, 2%/yr management, plus trading costs and funding inside NAV. Not investment advice. Not available where prohibited.
