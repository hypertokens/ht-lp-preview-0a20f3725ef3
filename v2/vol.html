Liquidation

You have no liquidation price of your own; the vault manages the margin. The vault's perp position can itself be liquidated by a fast enough move against it (roughly 17% at 6x), which would erase most or all of the token's value.

Sideways markets

Re-sizing has a cost when price chops, and NAV drifts down. Live on-chain NAV Oct 2–21 2026: iVOL −22.3%, VOL −26.4%, BTC −1.9%.

Margin reserve

The ~45¢ USDC margin reserve is working margin and is re-levered as the vault re-sizes. It does not protect value or lock in gains. Rebalancing is eligible, not guaranteed. Hedge figures are exposure at entry and drift as BTC moves.

Vault, access and fees

The vault owner (a Fordefi MPC multi-sig) can pause trading and upgrade code. $500k deposit cap per vault; trading and redemptions are subject to liquidity, vault limits and pauses, and sells above instant-sell capacity are refused.
