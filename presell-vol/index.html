How it works

Leverage isn't a setting.
It's a ratio that moves.

If you've never traded a perp: it's a contract that lets you hold about 6x the BTC you paid for, as long as enough margin stays in the account. Opening one is easy. Keeping it at 6x is the job — and that is the job VOL does for you.

1

You open a 6x perp

Your margin covers one-sixth of the position. At entry, that's 6x — and that is the last moment it is exactly 6x.

2

BTC moves 5%

Up 5%: the same short now runs 8.1x on less margin — and a 16.7% rise is a liquidation. Down 5%: 4.8x. Leverage rises as you lose and falls as you win.

3

VOL re-sizes toward ~6x

The vault adds or trims exposure so the token keeps targeting ~6x — eligible about every 4 hours or on 10% drift — and it posts the margin. You just have the token. No perps to manage yourself.

Before you buy

Know the risks.

Leveraged tokens can lose most or all of their value; no return is guaranteed.VOL is a leveraged product built for a move, not for parking money. Read the full risk disclosure →
  • Leverage. At ~6x, a 16.7% rise in BTC against the position is roughly a wipe-out of the vault's position. Emergency close can trigger at 20% free margin, with redemptions paused.
  • Liquidation. You have no liquidation price of your own, but the vault's perpetual can be liquidated by a fast enough adverse move.
  • Sideways markets. Re-sizing has a cost when price chops. NAV can fall even if BTC ends flat.
  • Fees and funding. Management, venue fees and funding accrue inside NAV, whether or not BTC moves your way.
  • Control and upgrades. Vault admin sits behind a Fordefi multi-sig (MPC) and contracts can be upgraded. Not trustless; not "can't be changed".
  • Bridges and venues. Cross-chain transfers and the derivatives venue carry their own risks.
Straight answers

FAQ

What's a perp, in one sentence?

A perpetual futures contract: a position that lets you hold a multiple of the BTC you paid for, as long as enough margin stays in the account — with no expiry, and a funding payment that passes between longs and shorts.

Is VOL the same as a 6x perp?

No. A perp is 6x at entry and then drifts with price. VOL targets ~6x and is re-sized back toward it; between re-sizes the ratio drifts too. Over any given holding period the two can return more or less than each other, so VOL is not "6x BTC returns" — it is a ~6x short target you don't have to maintain yourself.

Can I get liquidated?

You have no liquidation price of your own — the vault manages margin. The vault's own position can be liquidated by a fast enough adverse move (roughly 16.7% rise at 6x, earlier in a thin market), which would erase most or all of the token's value.

What does it cost?

Pool: 1% each way. Vault fallback: 0.1% to mint, 0.3% to redeem at NAV. Inside NAV: 2%/yr management, 0.1% builder fee, venue trading fees and funding. Bridge and gas are shown in each quote.

Who controls the vault?

Admin sits behind a Fordefi multi-sig (MPC). Contracts are upgradeable and the team can pause. The token is an ERC-4626 vault share on HyperEVM; the position lives on a derivatives venue.

How do I sell?

Sell into the USDC pool any time, or redeem at NAV from the vault. The vault keeps a USDC reserve for instant redemptions; in an emergency close, redemptions can pause.
