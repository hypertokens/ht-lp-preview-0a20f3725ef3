How it works: USDC → vault share → BTC perp

Pay with USDC from HyperEVM, Arbitrum, Base and more → USDC on HyperEVM. The token is an ERC-20 share of its ERC-4626 vault. Vault shares live on HyperEVM; the vault holds a BTC long perp on Hyperliquid (HyperCore).

Buys use the token’s USDC pool. When the pool is thin or above the pool maximum, mint at NAV (~0.1%), subject to each vault’s $500k deposit cap.

04 / OBJECTIONS TO TAKE SERIOUSLY

DELEGATED MARGIN.
REAL LOSS EXPOSURE.

Review the limits that remain before choosing a token.

RISK / 01

iVOL loses when BTC falls

At about 6x, a roughly 17% BTC drop can wipe out iVOL. Illustrative, before fees and funding. The vault can be liquidated earlier; 17% is not a safe threshold.

RISK / 02

Chop can erode iVOL

Repeated reversals can erode value even if BTC ends flat. Rebalancing, funding and fees affect the outcome. Rebalancing does not protect accumulated gains.

RISK / 03

The vault itself can be liquidated

No personal liquidation price applies to your token. The underlying leveraged vault can still be liquidated. A token balance does not protect its value.

RISK / 04

The owner can pause or upgrade

The vault owner (Fordefi MPC) can pause trading or upgrade the vault code. Pashov audited Ensemble vault code.

RISK / 05

Entry and exit have limits

Each vault has a $500k deposit cap. Pool depth and vault instant-sell capacity limit trades. Sells above vault capacity are refused, not queued; an exit may be unavailable.

RISK / 06

Execution and safeguards have limits

The circuit breaker limits transaction sizes and the deposit buffer. It does not prevent liquidation. The site halts trading if NAV reads 0, NAV is unreadable for more than 60 seconds, or maxDeposit is 0.

Leveraged tokens can lose most or all of their value; no return is guaranteed.

Only use an amount you can afford to lose entirely. Monitor the position and plan your exit. Rebalancing, an audit and a wallet balance do not protect you against loss.
05 / PERP TRADER FAQ

BEFORE YOU SWITCH
HOW YOU TRADE.

The ownership, leverage, costs and limits, in plain language.

Which BTC direction does this token target?

iVOL targets about 6x long BTC.

What am I buying?

An ERC-20 share of iVOL’s ERC-4626 vault on HyperEVM, holding a BTC long perp on Hyperliquid (HyperCore).

Does 6x mean six times BTC’s return over my holding period?

No. About 6x is a target at a point in time. Leverage drifts between rebalances. Choppy markets lose money even if BTC ends flat (path dependence/decay). Fees and funding affect NAV.

Do I have a personal liquidation price?

No. The vault manages margin and can be liquidated, erasing most or all of its value.

When does the vault rebalance?

About every 4h or after 10% drift from target leverage, each vault becomes eligible. Eligibility is not a fixed execution schedule.

Who controls the vault?

The vault owner (Fordefi MPC) can pause trading or upgrade the vault code. Vault fees go to Ensemble, which shares an owner with Hyper Tokens.

Can I sell whenever I want?

Pool liquidity and vault instant-sell capacity limit exits. Sells above vault capacity are refused, not queued. The vault owner can pause trading.

What happens if the pool is thin?

Buys and sells use a USDC pool on HyperEVM. If the pool is thin or above its maximum, the route falls back to minting at NAV (~0.1%) or redeeming at NAV (~0.3%), subject to vault limits.

What fees apply?

Pool/vault route fees, management, rebalance trades and funding affect value. Costs we know of and fee recipients.
